The problem

Your health lives in four different apps, and none of them sees your whole day.

Four apps, no single picture

A food tracker, a workout log, a wearable app, and guesswork for the meals you share. None of them talk to each other.

The same target every day

A desk day and a training day get the same calorie number. Fixed plans ignore how life actually varies.

Coaching that looks backward

Most apps summarise what you already did. Real coaching should tell you what to eat next and how hard to train tomorrow.

Built for one person

Families and couples share meals while needing different nutrition. Most apps are built for one person, not one table.

Questions and answers

What is Fit-Check?

Fit-Check is a health and fitness tracker. It logs your meals, tracks your workouts, pulls in your Apple Health data, keeps your household in sync and coaches your whole day, all in one app.

Is it a fitness tracker or a nutrition app?

Both. Fit-Check tracks food, workouts and activity together, so your coaching is based on your whole day and not only on what you ate.

Is it free?

Yes. Fit-Check is free while it is in early access, with no account needed. A paid premium tier is planned for later; core logging will stay free.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens in your browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it behaves like an app.

Does it work with my watch or band?

It syncs with Apple Health today, so steps, workouts and weight feed into your targets. Fitbit and other bands work by turning on their Apple Health sync. Direct Garmin and Whoop connections are on the roadmap, not live yet.

Can my partner or family use it with me?

Yes. Create a household, share the code, and a shared meal is split per person, for however many people you live with, even when everyone has a different goal.

How accurate is a photo?

It is an estimate, and it says what it assumed. You can correct the portion in a few words, and saved kitchen items use exact label numbers.

How is Fit-Check different from other health trackers?

Most trackers do food, or fitness, or wearables. Fit-Check puts all three in one place with coaching that looks at your whole day, and it splits shared meals automatically, which single-user trackers do not.

What happens to my data?

Meal photos and coach questions are sent to Anthropic to be read and answered. Nothing else leaves your phone unless you share a household. The full note is on the privacy page.
